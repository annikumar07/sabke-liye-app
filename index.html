      🧑‍🏫 For Grown-ups

      Sabke Liye follows Universal Design for Learning: many ways to take in information, many ways to respond, and many ways to stay engaged. Here is how each tool can support children with different needs.

      🗣️ Speech & communication

      The Talk Board is a simple AAC (Augmentative and Alternative Communication) board in English and Hindi.

      • Model it yourself: tap the pictures as you speak.
      • Accept every attempt at communication.
      • Start with “I want”, “help”, “break” and “stop”.

      🖐️ Movement & motor needs

      • Every button is large, with no dragging or double-tapping.
      • Switch scanning lights pictures one by one; any single press (Space, a big button, or an adaptive switch mapped to Space) chooses.
      • Nothing is timed, so no one is rushed.

      📚 Dyslexia & reading

      • Turn on Easy-read font and Extra line spacing.
      • Try a coloured page tint in Read With Me.
      • Paste your own worksheet so the child hears it while reading along.

      👀 Low vision

      • High contrast mode and text up to 3 sizes bigger.
      • Works with screen readers: labelled buttons, headings and live updates.
      • Everything can be done with the keyboard.

      🦻 Deaf & hard of hearing

      • Captions show every spoken word on screen.
      • Feedback is always visual (colour, stars, text) as well as sound.
      • Pair the pictures with your local signs.

      🧠 Autism, ADHD & sensory needs

      • Soft colours and Stop movement reduce sensory load.
      • First–Then and the visual timer make changes predictable.
      • One line at a time cuts visual clutter while reading.
      • Calm Corner gives a planned, shame-free break.

      ⌨️ Keyboard help

      Tab move to the next button · Enter or Space press it · Esc stop talking and close settings · Space choose a picture while switch scanning is on

      Voices come from the device. For Hindi speech, a Hindi voice must be installed (most Android phones and Windows/Chrome have one). Settings are saved only in this browser.
